THE AXIS FLIP
Arena y counts upward from (0,0) at bottom-left. Android canvas y counts downward. Every draw inverts: screenY = (19 − cellY) × cell.
That inversion lives in one function in arena/, unit-tested on the JVM. Scattering it is how grids end up mirrored.
CONFIRM WITH THE SUPERVISOR
The robot is 3×3 cells, so ROBOT,7,2 could name its bottom-left cell or its centre. The algorithms briefing recommends bottom-left, but its other sanctioned representation explicitly allows the centre — and the ARCM slide just prints “Robot (7, 2)” without saying which.
We draw bottom-left. If the RPi means centre, everything sits one cell off diagonally — cheap to fix, expensive to discover during a run.
