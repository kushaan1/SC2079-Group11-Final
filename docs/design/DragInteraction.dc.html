WHY THE HIT AREA IS BIGGER THAN THE BLOCK
On an 8.7″ screen a grid cell is 4.7 mm. Material’s minimum touch target is 48dp — 7.6 mm. The block cannot be its own target.
So hit-testing is decoupled from rendering: the block draws at one cell, but claims a 48dp radius. Overlaps resolve to the nearest centre.
